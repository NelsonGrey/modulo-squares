Modulo Squares turns divisibility into a fast, strategic arcade puzzle.

A number falls. Ten buckets wait. Guide the tile left or right, then drop it onto a bucket that divides it evenly. A clean match grows your score and combo. A remainder costs points. The red Dead bucket costs the falling number, so every decision matters as the numbers grow and the timer speeds up.

HOW TO PLAY
• Move the falling number left or right across ten lanes
• Choose a bucket that divides the number evenly
• Drop early when you know the answer
• Build combos to increase your movement speed
• Fill the 10 × 10 progress grid to reach the next level
• Adapt as the number range expands and the fall timer accelerates

FEATURES

• A persistent local best score to chase
• Clear scoring, combo, speed, range, and progress feedback

Modulo Squares is made for puzzle fans, number-game players, and anyone who wants arithmetic practice to feel like an arcade challenge instead of a worksheet.
