## Publishing notes

- Set visibility to Public or Unlisted; Google Play accepts either.
- Allow embedding and turn monetization off for any video linked from the Play Store listing.
- Use the existing `Modulo Squares — Official Gameplay Trailer` as the Play Console promo-video URL. The tutorials belong on the channel and support/help surfaces rather than replacing the concise listing trailer.
- Review automatic captions even though these masters are silent; upload a manually written description and, if voice-over is added later, a corrected caption track.
- The email and password shown in the capture are illustrative local-only values. No real account is created and no credentials are sent to Firebase.
