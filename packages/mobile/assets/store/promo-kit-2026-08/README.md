# Modulo Squares store promotion kit

Ready-to-upload Apple App Store, Google Play, YouTube, and social media materials for Modulo Squares. The content reflects the current falling-number divisor-bucket game and deliberately makes no leaderboard, daily-challenge, Game Center, or retired board-clearing claims.

The production rules were verified on 2026-08-03. Review [`specifications.md`](specifications.md) and recheck the linked official sources if this kit is submitted substantially later.

`asset-manifest.csv` is generated by the validator. It records dimensions, media type, color/alpha state, codec, frame rate, duration, file size, and pass/fail status for every publication deliverable. Raw native captures and working files under `sources/` are intentionally excluded from the publication manifest.

## Upload order

### Apple App Store Connect

1. Build and upload the app with the canonical 1024 × 1024 icon in its asset catalog. The PNG in this kit is a source/reference; it is not a separate product-page upload field.
2. Upload the six screenshots from `apple/screenshots/iphone-6.9/` in filename order to the 6.9-inch iPhone slot.
3. Upload the portrait MP4 from `apple/app-preview/` as the first iPhone app preview and choose a representative poster frame in App Store Connect.
4. Apply the fields in `copy/apple-en-US.md`, then verify the support, marketing, and privacy-policy URLs in the live browser.
5. Attach `apple/in-app-purchase/remove-ads-1024x1024.png` to the `remove_ads` in-app purchase only if that purchase is being promoted on the product page. Confirm the displayed price and availability in App Store Connect; neither is baked into the artwork.

There is no general Apple feature-graphic or banner upload. The app currently targets iPhone only, so this package does not include iPad screenshots or previews.

### Google Play Console and YouTube

1. Upload the icon and feature graphic from `google/icon/` and `google/feature-graphic/`.
2. Upload the six files in `google/screenshots/phone/` in filename order. Copy the matching accessible descriptions from `copy/google-en-US.md`.
3. Apply the app name, short description, full description, website, support, and privacy-policy fields from `copy/google-en-US.md`.
4. Upload `google/video/modulo-squares-youtube-promo-1920x1080.mp4` to the official YouTube channel as public or unlisted. Allow embedding, turn monetization/ads off for the listing video, and use the title, description, and tags in the copy file.
5. Paste the final direct YouTube watch URL into Play Console with no timecode, playlist, or tracking parameters. Confirm the first frame and feature-graphic treatment in the listing preview.

The final console uploads, YouTube publication, localized URL checks, pricing verification, rights confirmation, and store-review submission require an authorized account owner and are not performed by this repository workflow.

## Conditional assets not included

- iPad media: the Xcode target is currently iPhone-only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`).
- Google 7-inch and 10-inch tablet media: create these after native tablet layout and input QA, and only when those devices are intentionally distributed.
- Android TV, Wear OS, Automotive, Chromebook-specific, and Android XR art: no corresponding product target is configured.
- Apple editorial artwork: create it only in response to an Apple Promotional Asset Request and its supplied template.
- Google Play Promotional Content event media: evergreen launch art does not satisfy the event, offer, major-update, or new-content requirement.
- Google’s retired 180 × 120 promo graphic: it is not a current main-listing field.

## Production provenance

- Store screenshots and gameplay footage use the local-only `lib/main_store_capture.dart` harness. That entry point supplies stable capture data and skips authentication, ads, analytics, and network activity; it does not replace the release app entry point. For video, `STORE_CAPTURE_EXPERT_DEMO=true` enables a default-off expert driver that moves through the real production controls, always selects the highest-scoring valid divisor, and produces an uninterrupted no-miss run.
- The app icon, Play icon, feature graphic, and in-app-purchase image derive from the repository’s established Modulo Squares artwork.
- The cross-platform key-art master was produced with OpenAI’s built-in image generator in reference-guided generation mode, using the canonical icon and current feature graphic. Production prompt: “Create a polished 16:9 ads-and-marketing key-art master for Modulo Squares. Show the number 18 falling toward divisor buckets 3, 6, and 9 on a glowing blue modular grid, with 6 highlighted as the selected bucket. Keep generous dark-blue negative space on the left for copy. Use crisp premium vector-like game art with electric cyan and purple accents. No words, logos, device frame, or unrelated game elements.” The unlettered generated source is retained as `sources/key-art-master-generated.png`.
- Final screenshots, social stills, and six 1920 × 1080 video plates are deterministic HTML/CSS compositions from `scripts/store-promo/render.html`, using the native captures and generated key art rather than synthetic UI. The plate sources are retained in `sources/video/plates/`.
- `scripts/store-promo/capture-ios-expert-gameplay.sh` builds and installs the capture target, records a deterministic expert run from a booted iOS Simulator, and replaces the raw source only after the recording completes successfully.
- `scripts/store-promo/render-videos.sh` creates the silent 16.9-second Apple preview from one continuous section of that expert run. It then places the preview at a 1000-pixel height over six equal-duration branded plates for the Google/YouTube master and derives three 5.5-second vertical clips with blurred edge fill.
- Editorial intent, scene order, and on-screen copy are documented in `copy/video-production-script.md`.

## Rebuild and validation

Run from the repository root. ImageMagick, FFmpeg/FFprobe, Flutter, and Node/Playwright are the production dependencies. Playwright is declared in the repository's root `package.json` (not a private, machine-local install), so a fresh checkout only needs:

```bash
npm install
npx playwright install --with-deps chromium
```

Then rebuild and validate:

```bash
./scripts/store-promo/capture-ios-expert-gameplay.sh
./scripts/store-promo/render-assets.sh
./scripts/store-promo/render-videos.sh
python3 scripts/store-promo/validate-assets.py
```

The asset renderer rebuilds the six Apple screenshots, six Google screenshots, three cross-platform social stills, and six branded video plates from the checked-in native captures, driving Chromium through the repository-managed `scripts/store-promo/capture.mjs` (see `scripts/store-promo/render.html`'s inline script for the load-failure contract that script enforces). It also copies the six Apple screenshots into `packages/mobile/ios/fastlane/screenshots/en-US/`, which is what the `verify_metadata`/`submit_to_app_store` Fastlane lanes actually upload. The video renderer then rebuilds the Apple preview, Google/YouTube upload master, and three social clips from those local sources.

Both renderers record a content hash of each deliverable's source inputs to `packages/mobile/assets/store/promo-kit-2026-08/render-provenance.json` (see `scripts/store-promo/render_provenance.py`). The validator always rewrites `packages/mobile/assets/store/promo-kit-2026-08/asset-manifest.csv`; it exits non-zero for missing or stale deliverables (i.e. a deliverable whose recorded source hashes no longer match `render.html`, the native capture, or the video/plate it was actually built from), wrong dimensions or type, disallowed alpha channels, non-sRGB/incorrect bit-depth images, video codec/pixel-format/container mismatches, excessive frame rate or size, and invalid duration. If you intentionally hand-edit a deliverable outside these scripts (or are adopting the staleness check on an already-current kit), run `python3 scripts/store-promo/validate-assets.py --bootstrap-provenance` to record the current sources as the new baseline -- it does not rebuild or validate anything.

To validate a copied or staged kit, supply its path explicitly:

```bash
python3 scripts/store-promo/validate-assets.py \
  --kit-root /absolute/path/to/promo-kit-2026-08 \
  --manifest /absolute/path/to/promo-kit-2026-08/asset-manifest.csv
```

The media masters are retained in the kit so routine copy or still-image revisions do not require transcoding video. If native gameplay or Simulator launch timing changes, run the capture script, confirm that the 7.5-second edit point lands on the in-app rules overlay immediately before play, then rerun both renderers and the validator before upload. The scene intent and expert-play acceptance checks remain documented in `copy/video-production-script.md`.
